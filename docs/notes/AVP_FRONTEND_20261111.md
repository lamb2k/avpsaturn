## 1. Issue 7, select screen name plates (done, data only, 0 B RAM)

Which layer draws what, from the arcade's own state at the select (MAME frame 1760, the three CPS tilemaps rendered one at a time with `tools/extract_avspu_stage1.py`, `layers.py`): the silver frames are scroll 3, the four portraits scroll 2, and the name plates, stat bars, texts and the countdown are scroll 1. The Saturn scales scroll 2 and 3 by 11/12 (VDP2) and cannot scale scroll 1, which keeps its 8 pixel cells and drops 4 of 48 columns (`home_row_map`, plate rows 0, 22, 25, 47: none inside a plate). So a plate (160 px, columns 1-20 and 27-46) stayed 160 px wide in a frame that had shrunk to 147: the left plates stuck out at the left, the right plates at the right (`ev\plate_before_after.png`, `ev\plate_prev_*.png` is the geometry model).

Plates start at arcade x 8 (left) and 216 (right) and sit at Saturn x 0 and 192 (cells 0-19 and 24-43), so the two sides get different offsets. Applies to the four stock plates (Warrior 0x0e00, Schaefer 0x0e30, Hunter 0x0e60, Linn 0x0e90; palettes read from the ROM tables 0x1ee360, 0x1ee340, 0x1ee300, 0x1ee320, tags checked) and Wolverine's (his PNG pens). No code, no RAM table: `build.py` gives the arcade's front file (row 0) a graphics region with the stock plates fitted (`gen\hud\gfx_front_plates.bin`) and `hud_art.build(fit=True)` does the same for `front_wv`. `PLATE_FIT=0` builds the old plates.

## 2. Issue 9, option menu text over the logo (three mock-ups, not shipped)

Cause as described: `home_cell` computes the halo inside each 8x8 cell, so a letter dot on a cell edge gets no ring dot in the neighbour cell. All three are default-off switches in sat_gfx.c (`-DSAT_HOME_OPT=1|2|3`, commit caf7255 and follow-up); none changes the build without the flag. Captures of the same five screens (paused, `ev\opt\<tag>_<screen>.png`, sheets `ev\opt_sheet_*.png`, zoom `ev\opt_zoom_gameopt.png`).

| Mock-up | What it does | hw RAM | lw RAM | cart | VRAM |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (a) full halo | each cell of an option screen is built from the 3x3 cells around it, so the ring crosses cell edges; a pool of cells holds the variants | 976 B as built (432 free -> -544: does not link). Moved to cold code like the stage-menu-bg save: about 20-40 B resident, +about 1.1 KB cart | 0 | about +1.1 KB if moved cold | pool of 64 cells = 2 KB used out of the 880 scroll 1 slots (largest use over the five screens 49 cells; the mock reserves 200 = 6.4 KB) |
| (b) dimmed logo | VDP2 colour offset A of -80 on every screen but the menu layer, only while an option screen is up | 40 B (432 -> 392) | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| (c) panel behind the text rows | one band of the pause panel (translucent dark, ratio 11 of 31) from one cell before the first letter to one after the last in each row that has text; letter cells carry the panel too | 120 B (432 -> 312; first version without the letter cells 128 B) | 0 | 0 | 0 (the panel cell exists) |

Notes for the choice: (a) is the one that fixes the cause (each letter keeps its whole outline) but its effect is small on its own over the bright orange; (b) and (c) are the ones that move readability. (b) also darkens the "ALIEN" glow and the PREDATOR letters (the owner sees a dim logo behind option screens only); (c) is visible as bands. The panel is translucent, so the ratio register decides how dark: `sat_home_ratio` (default 11); a lower ratio is darker (captures at 11 and at 5, `c_` and `c5_`; ratio 20 is lighter, `c20_` is the first version without the letter cells). Numbers: (a) was captured on a SAT_HOME_FONT_COLD build (frees 1,920 B of hw RAM so it links): FONT_COLD alone is 2,352 free (432 + 1,920), (a) on it 1,384, so (a) costs 968 B there; the 8 B difference from 976 is layout.

## 3. Issue 8, an alien body vanishing in the opening street scene (classification)

Result: not reproduced on this head; the evidence rules out the three candidates for it, and the one case that did match the report (3a's shadow-only moment) was Schaefer, not an alien (section below).

Method (Yaba, normal play and paused `framebuffer_burst`, MAME reference): the standard build already counts what SAT_KEYLOG and SAT_VLOG would show for this question, so those two diagnostic builds were not made: `sat_gfx_stats.missing` counts every key the game asked for that is not in the loaded round file (what SAT_KEYLOG reads back), and `refused_*` and the per-picture counters give capacity and cadence (SAT_VLOG).
- Normal play, owner recipe, ARCADE MODE from the title (`ev\intro_base`): the street is stage word mode 2, st8 2 for about 225 vblanks; missing 0 and every refusal counter 0 throughout (missing first rises later, in the cutscene and the select, as before: 413).
- Stepped, 260 frames from the street's start (`ev\burst_base`, first 3 frames dropped), and again with Wolverine switched on at the menu (`ev\burst_guest`): each Saturn frame matched to the nearest of 131 MAME street frames (`mame_street.lua`, every 2nd frame, the arcade object list every frame); mean difference 8 to 12 of 255 in every frame and no outlier except the scene cuts and the first stepped frames; left-edge strip (where the aliens enter) and centre strip tested separately, same result. No frame shows a shadow without its body.
- The arcade's own list (`mame_street\obj.bin`): object count rises 12 to 61 smoothly over the scene with no one-frame dropout of a body; the only alternating objects are the two-frame shadow animation (codes 0xf9f8 and 0xf9f0, palette 12).
- Held pictures: plots run at about 1.9 vblanks (136 plots in 260 vblanks); of 260 stepped frames 94 repeat the previous picture, runs of 1 to 6. A held picture is a whole old picture, bodies included, so it cannot show a shadow alone; and `commands` stays under 250 of a 1,000 table.

The 3a frames (`E:\AvP\learning\lane-stage-sprites\intro-3a`, `street_trio.png`): what is missing there is Schaefer, the big man with the launcher, for the whole street scene: his shadow stays, Linn and the aliens at the left are drawn. That is not a brief vanish and not an alien. 3a is the sprite-skin Wolverine disc (guest on by default), so the guest remap was the suspect (`wolverine.c` `is_wolverine()` tested the player block's byte +1, which the street's Schaefer also has; the native lane moved to +0xd2). A 3a-like build from this head's sources (`build_3a.sh`: `SAT_GUEST_DEFAULT=1`, no native, D11 assets, rowtrim 2; hw_free 316, lw_free 72, cart_free 6,552; `sat_guest_sel` reads 1 at the menu) does not reproduce it either: Schaefer is drawn in all 260 stepped frames (`evurst_3a`, `burst_3a_contact.png`), missing 0, no refusals. So whatever 3a showed is already gone on this line (the stage-sprites final keys 9298450 and the native lane both landed after 3a), and the cause was not confirmed. If the owner sees a body drop again: the readout to take at that moment is `sat_gfx_stats.missing` and `refused_*` (any build, over MCP or SAT_FPS_HUD); a rising `missing` is the missing-keys class (data fix, `CHAR_FUZZ_KEY_STAGES` / front keys), flat counters with a shadow alone are the arcade's list or VDP1 timing, which these runs did not show.
